Elvis Escape 🐺

You are Elvis, a pure-white Siberian husky and an absolute menace. Every time the humans leave, it's your job to break out of the room. Shove things into place, climb, tip furniture, and paw the levers to crack open that sliding door. Grow from a 3-month-old puppy into a 4-year-old escape artist across 8 rooms.

One husky. Eight rooms. A lifetime of escapes (and a suspicious number of missing socks).
